## What This Trainer Does

AQ40 Ouro Skip Trainer provides a browser simulation for rehearsing the trash skip route inside the Temple of Ahn'Qiraj. The exercise covers the movement from the Twin Emperors' room toward Ouro's room, giving players a way to repeat the route without setting up the complete encounter.

The scenario includes both fixed and patrolling trash groups, as well as detection and pursuit behavior after aggro. Its movement rules are tuned to World of Warcraft values and include several hunter-focused options: Feign Death, Potion of Invisibility, and Aspect of the Cheetah.
